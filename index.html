SVG Slimmer

Traces a logo or icon, SVG or bitmap, as smooth curves, colour by colour, or tidies an SVG that's already well traced, then checks the result against the original pixel by pixel so you can see what, if anything, was lost.

Open an SVG or PNG or drop it here, or paste SVG code
Method

Redraw traces the artwork again as long smooth curves: best for bloated exports and traces made of thousands of tiny segments. Tidy keeps the SVG's own outlines and only merges near-identical colours, joins shapes and shortens the code: best when the outlines are already good, as in a trace from VTracer.

Quality

Sets detail, smoothing and small-shape protection together. Adjust any of them below.

    Colours closer than this are treated as one. It's a distance between colours, where black to white is 441: around 16 merges shades you can't tell apart, 80 merges clearly different ones, such as cream into white. Fewer colours make a smaller file.

    AI redrawOpenRouter

    Asks an AI model to rebuild the artwork the way a designer would, from simple shapes, gradients and glows, then shows it its drawing next to the original and asks it to fix what's wrong. Often far smaller and cleaner than a trace, but less exact. Sends the image to OpenRouter and the model's maker, and costs a few cents a round on your own OpenRouter account.

    From openrouter.ai/keys. Kept on this device only and sent nowhere but OpenRouter.

    Rough cost of one round. Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-6.1 Sol came out closest in testing; Haiku is far cheaper and can do for a simple logo.

    Sends the model a quick trace too, so it can read off exact positions and colours. Much closer on flat logos and lettering; worse on soft lighting and glows, and dearer when the trace is big.

    SVG code

    Works on SVGs and on bitmap images (PNG, JPEG, WebP), and best on flat artwork: logos, icons and illustrations with a handful of solid colours. Gradients and soft shadows are reduced to the nearest solid colour, and text is traced as shapes. Differences are measured at 2048 pixels on the long side, and an edge that moved by less than one pixel there doesn't count.

    Tracing by Potrace (Peter Selinger), ported to Rust from the JavaScript port by kilobtye.
